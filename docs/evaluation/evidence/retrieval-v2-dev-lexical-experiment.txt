# Decision: V3. Mechanism (not score) is the justification: with OR queries every occurrence of any
# word is a "cover" for ts_rank_cd, so it degenerates to occurrence counting and favours long,
# stopword-dense chunks; ts_rank's OR path saturates per-term frequency and averages over terms.
# V5 is better on dev but adds a per-query document-frequency round trip and new SQL construction;
# recorded as a recommendation (IDF/BM25), not adopted. 6 variants were compared on dev only.
